Setup & Calibration

Before installing any servo end effectors, power up the system and go to the setup position. Then install the end effectors as follows:
  1. Install the pusher in the catch position using the circular servo horn, with the shuttle cork interrupting the through-beam sensor.
  2. Install the gripper servo horn (24 mm) in the closed position. Ensure the linkage screws do not interfere with the tower.
  3. Install the turret rotation servo horn (24 mm) radially, pointing away from the servo.
  4. Mount the trajectory linkage horizontally at the front-most position using the circular servo horn.

ESC Calibration

Keep clear of the motors and ensure they can spin freely in case calibration or speed control fails. After successful calibration the motors remain stationary and you will hear the beeps described below. Motors must be connected to hear tones. These instructions are for an RC Electric Parts 30A ESC; follow your ESC's instructions if it differs.
Throttle Range Calibration
  1. Disconnect the electronic speed controls from the battery.
  2. Go to Max Throttle.
  3. Connect the electronic speed control you want to calibrate.
  4. Before the four beeps finish, go to Min Throttle.
  5. After a few seconds, the motor will beep twice briefly and then twice longer to confirm the throttle range is set.
  6. Disconnect the ESC to lock the settings.
Brake Calibration
  1. Start the system normally and check the brake tone. Three beeps indicate a 3-cell battery, followed by one beep if the brake is on or two beeps if it is off. Continue only if the brake needs to be turned on.
  2. Disconnect the electronic speed controls from the battery.
  3. Use the button below to go to Max Throttle.
  4. Connect the electronic speed control you want to calibrate.
  5. Wait for the first four short beeps to finish.
  6. During the next four groups of three rapid beeps, go to Min Throttle using the button below. This toggles the brake setting.
  7. After a few seconds, the motor will beep twice briefly and then twice longer to confirm the brake is on.
  8. Disconnect the ESC to lock the settings.

RPM Calibration

After setting the ESC throttle range, use a tachometer to calibrate RPM. Test low and high speeds and enter the measured values. After saving, test the motor speeds to verify them.

Instructions

The ACE shuttlecock launcher is a badminton trainer that can be configured for many different patterns. This section explains basic operation.

Basic Operation:
  1. Select a preset. It loads shots that you can use as-is or adjust in the next steps.
  2. Select a play type. Play Once runs the selected pattern once. Repeat runs it continuously. Mix Order randomly selects a pattern entry for each shot, so entries can repeat. Random Shots selects from all loaded shots and ignores the pattern.
  3. Adjust the preset shots for your training needs. See Adjusting Shots for details.
  4. Build a shot pattern by tapping or clicking the court markers in the desired order. Shots use the Default Delay slider value. To change an individual delay, tap or click its grey delay marker in the pattern.
  5. When the pattern is ready, press the play button to start feeding and the stop button to stop.
Navigation:
  • Use two fingers to pan the court. On a PC, right-click and drag.
  • Use two fingers to zoom the court. On a PC, use the scroll wheel.
  • Drag with one finger in an area without a shot marker or button to rotate the court. On a PC, left-click and drag.
Adjusting Shots:
  1. Press and drag a court shot marker to its desired position. Its parameters appear in the editing controls and the other shots fade.
  2. Adjust trajectory to get the desired flight path.
  3. Adjust rotation to control the launcher's physical rotation. Wheel Correction shows the angle the system will try to achieve using the difference between wheel speeds. Minimize this value for the best accuracy.
  4. Red shots are unreachable with the current angle and rotation. The launcher may be aimed too far from the landing point, or the trajectory may be too high or low to cover the distance.
